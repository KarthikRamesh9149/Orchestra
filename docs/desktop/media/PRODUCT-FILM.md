### Recording details

Socrates and Deep Research show saved, real OpenAI output from the sample project, reopened in the new app. The review, approval, document navigation and Preflight were performed for this recording. The edit is not a continuous screen recording or a latency benchmark.

The voiceover is AI-generated from the written scripts using Kokoro’s `am_michael` voice. No microphone or user audio was used. The films include captions and are encoded at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps. [File sizes and SHA-256 checksums](narrated-2026-11-19/media-manifest.json) identify the exact web editions.

The [September recording notes](narrated-2026-09-22/PROVENANCE.md) and [earlier evidence-only demos](EVIDENCE-DEMO.md) describe previous editions. The films demonstrate the source preview; they are not downloadable installers.
